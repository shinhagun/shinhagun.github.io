Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Lottery Results Live (“the app”, “we”) shows the winning numbers, prizes, draw schedules, archive and descriptive statistics of lottery draws in the United States, France, Germany, Italy and Spain, and tools to check your own tickets. This policy explains which data the app processes, why, on what legal basis and for how long, under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the privacy laws of US states. The app is not an official app and is not offered by any lottery operator.

Summary

  • No account, no name, no email address, no phone number: the app only uses an anonymous device ID.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are off by default and only start if you opt in.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google asks for your consent or offers your opt-out choices before ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform); on iPhone the app also asks whether tracking is allowed. VIP subscribers see no ads.
  • Saved tickets, budget, claim reminders and settings stay on your device. Tickets are only sent to our server for the automatic check (VIP).

Data kept only on your device

Saved tickets (except for automatic checking, see above), monthly budget and limits, prize-claim reminders (local notifications), the countries and games you follow, display settings, the results cache and your privacy choices stay on your device. Numbers you type to check a ticket are not transmitted. Deleting the app removes this data.

Widget

The home-screen widget only loads public results and draw times from our server. It sends no personal data, no device ID and no tickets; only the IP address is processed for technical reasons (see “Technical access data”).

What we do not collect

No precise location, contacts, photos or payment data (Apple handles payments). Firebase Analytics does not collect the advertising ID.

Recipients and international transfers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Installations, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Hosting, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config and — only with your consent — Analytics and Crashlytics) and, for users without VIP, Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform. For users in the EEA and Switzerland the provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; otherwise Google LLC, USA.

Our databases are located in the United States (Google Cloud, us-east1); some server functions that collect official results run in the EU. Data of EEA, UK and Swiss users is therefore transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR) and the UK and Swiss extensions; in addition, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply (Art. 46 GDPR).

App Store purchases are processed by Apple (in the EU: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Cork, Ireland); Apple’s privacy policy also applies.

We do not sell personal data for money and do not pass it on to lottery operators or retailers.

Your choices

In the app under Me › Privacy you can turn usage statistics and crash reports on or off at any time and open “Ad privacy choices” to change your advertising consent or opt out. On iPhone, tracking is managed under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Notifications can be turned off under Me › Notifications or in the system settings.

Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). As we only know you by the anonymous device ID, please quote it in requests (Me › About: tap the version number seven times to show it).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)
Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f): technical access data, online configuration, ad fraud prevention), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop, unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds or the processing serves legal claims. An informal email to the address below is enough.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in your country of residence — for example the CNIL (France), your federal state’s authority or the BfDI (Germany), the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Italy) or the AEPD (Spain).

Your privacy rights (US state laws)

Depending on your state (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and others), you may have the right to know which personal information we process, to access, correct or delete it, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information and of targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money; ads shown by Google AdMob may count as “sharing” or targeted advertising — opt out in the app under Me › Privacy › Ad privacy choices or by not allowing tracking on iPhone. We do not process sensitive personal information and do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. To make a request or appeal a decision, email us; we may ask for the anonymous device ID to identify your data.

Automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Automatic ticket checking only compares your numbers with the official winning numbers.

Minors

Lottery games are only allowed for adults (18+; some US states 21+). The app is not directed at minors and does not knowingly collect their data.
