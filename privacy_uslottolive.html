Lotto Results Live

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Lotto Results Live ("the app", "we") shows results, history and statistics for US multi-state lottery games. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is offered in the United States only.

Summary

  • No account, name, email or phone number is needed. The app signs in with an anonymous ID.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are off by default and sent only if you turn them on.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not show personalized ads. VIP subscribers see no ads.
  • Saved tickets and preferences stay on your device. Only if you are a VIP subscriber, have notifications on and save tickets, are those tickets sent to our server so they can be checked automatically after each draw.
  • The app does not collect advertising identifiers.

Information the app uses

Anonymous app ID

When you first open the app it creates an anonymous ID with Google Firebase Authentication. We use it only to let the app read results from our servers, to remember a VIP subscription for your device, and to protect our services from abuse. It is not linked to your name or contact details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a push token and subscribes it to the games you choose (for example "Powerball results"). We use it only to send result notifications. You can change games or turn notifications off in Settings › Notifications or in your device settings; turning all games off unregisters the device.

VIP subscription

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. When you subscribe or restore, the app sends the store's signed transaction to our server, which verifies it with the store and records the subscription status and expiry date for your anonymous ID. Renewals, cancellations and refunds are reported to us by the store so your status stays correct.

Automatic ticket checking (VIP)

If you have an active VIP subscription, allow notifications and save tickets in the ticket checker, the app sends those saved tickets (the numbers and the options you chose, such as Power Play, Double Play or the multiplier printed on the ticket), your push token and your anonymous ID to our server. The server compares them with each new draw and sends a notification to your device if your saved numbers match a prize. We keep these saved tickets only while automatic checking is on: they are deleted when you remove your saved tickets, turn off notifications, or your VIP subscription ends (the next time you open the app), and when the push token stops working. A record that a match notification was sent for a draw is kept for 30 days to avoid duplicate notifications, then deleted.

Optional usage statistics and crash reports

Only if you turn on "Share anonymous usage & crash reports" (at first launch or in Settings › Privacy), the app sends Google Firebase Analytics events about which screens and features are used, and Firebase Crashlytics reports when the app crashes. These include device model, OS version, app version and the anonymous ID, but no name, contacts, location or ticket numbers. You can turn this off at any time.

Data stored only on your device

Your saved tickets (unless automatic ticket checking is on, see above), chosen games, theme, the results history cache and your privacy choices are stored on your device and are not uploaded. Numbers you enter to check a ticket are not uploaded.

What we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts or photos. The app does not collect advertising identifiers (it does not request tracking permission on iOS, and advertising ID collection is turned off on Android). We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Installations, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, and, only with your consent, Analytics and Crashlytics) to run the app. Firebase Installations and Remote Config use an app instance ID to deliver app settings; they are needed for the app to work and do not include your usage. Google processes this data on our behalf under its terms. Lottery results come from public sources and contain no personal data.

Retention and deletion

Subscription records (status, expiry date and the store's transaction IDs) are kept while they are needed to provide VIP and to handle renewals, refunds and disputes, and are deleted on request. Saved tickets for automatic checking are kept as described above. Analytics data, if enabled, is kept for up to 14 months. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. To ask us to delete server data linked to your anonymous ID, email us at shinhagun1@gmail.com and include the Device ID shown when you tap the version number in Settings › About seven times.

Your privacy rights (US state laws)

Depending on your state (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and others), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, to access or correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information or use it for targeted advertising. To exercise your rights, email us at shinhagun1@gmail.com; we will respond within the time the law requires and will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Children

The app is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Lottery games are for adults only.
